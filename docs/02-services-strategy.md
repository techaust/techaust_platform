## 2. What the market says (summary of the research)

1. **Demand is growing, but buyers want production results, not "AI".** Gartner forecasts AI spending up about 49.5% in 2026 and IT services as the largest IT segment (about $1.86T). Upwork reports "AI integration" demand up 178% year on year. Yet 42% of companies abandoned most AI initiatives in 2025 (S&P), and Gartner expects more than 40% of agentic projects to be cancelled by 2027. **Implication:** sell measurable outcomes, fixed scope, and a path to production with guardrails.
2. **AI coding tools squeeze hourly billing, not project value.** Coding is roughly 20% of project effort (vendor estimate), and agencies are moving to fixed and value-based pricing. **Implication:** price by package and outcome, and sell AI-assisted speed as faster delivery rather than a discount.
3. **Most durable demand for a small senior studio:**
   - Custom web apps, portals and internal tools (35% of enterprises have replaced at least one SaaS tool with custom software).
   - Systems integration (in India: Tally, Zoho, GST, WhatsApp, Razorpay).
   - AI document processing for finance and logistics (back-office automation had the best ROI in MIT's study).
   - Maintenance and AMC retainers.
   - Fractional CTO work.
   - Hardening apps that were built quickly with AI tools.
4. **Most commoditised:** brochure websites (AI site builders, and ₹10k–50k Indian price floors), simple chatbots and FAQ bots, basic n8n flows, basic Shopify theme setups, and generic SEO.
5. **India has two time-limited openings:**
   - **DPDP Rules:** Consent Manager provisions from about Nov 2026; core obligations from **13 May 2027**; penalties up to ₹250 Cr. **VERIFY WITH CA/LEGAL.**
   - **GST e-invoicing:** mandatory above ₹5 Cr turnover, with a 30-day reporting rule at ₹10 Cr+. **VERIFY WITH CA.**

   There is also steady demand around WhatsApp Business (about 15M accounts in India) and UPI.
6. **Competition.** "AI-first" no longer stands out; nearly every Indian studio says it. Small competitors win with fixed prices, published price anchors, named or metric-backed proof, and offshore-trust signals (registered entity, IP transfer on payment, time-zone overlap). **The gap nobody small owns:** a *senior, founder-delivered studio that does both product engineering and back-office automation*, especially finance and accounting operations across Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero.
7. **Rate benchmarks (Clutch / UX Continuum, Sept 2026):** Indian firms charge $25–49/hr; US software firms $50–99/hr; US web agencies, Canada and Australia $100–149/hr. A senior Indian studio can price at the top of the Indian band and still be 40–60% below Western agencies.

#### S1 · Discovery Sprint (LEAD, entry offer)
- **What it is:** a 1–2 week paid engagement in two flavours:
  - **Automation Audit:** map workflows, check data readiness, produce an ROI-ranked roadmap, and add DPDP and risk notes.
  - **Product Blueprint:** scope, architecture, clickable prototype and a fixed quote.
- **Who buys:** SMB owners, finance and operations heads, founders unsure what to build first.
- **Why demand lasts:** buyers burned by failed AI pilots want a low-risk first step. Fixed-price audits are a standard market product ($4.5–15k in the West).
- **Skills:** ✅ (senior scoping and architecture).
- **Deliverables:** findings report, prioritised roadmap with ROI estimates, architecture sketch or clickable prototype, fixed-price proposal for phase 1.
- **Pricing:** fixed; **fee credited** against the build if the client proceeds within 60 days. India from ₹40k; international from $2k.

#### S2 · App Rescue & Production-Readiness Audit (NOW)
- **What it is:** a review of apps built quickly (AI-generated, freelancer-built or legacy), followed by a fixed-price hardening plan and fixes.
- **Who buys:** founders whose prototype (built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or a freelancer) is failing in production; SMBs with fragile legacy apps.
- **Why demand lasts:** AI-assisted code is growing fast and creating maintenance and security debt. Accountability can't be generated by AI.
- **Skills:** ✅ full-stack · 🟡 formal security testing (penetration testing) is out of scope unless a partner does it.
- **Deliverables:** code, security and performance audit report; risk-ranked fix list; optional fixed-price hardening sprint; handover to a care plan.
- **Pricing:** fixed audit (from ₹50k / $2k) + fixed hardening sprint.

#### S3 · DPDP Readiness for Apps (NOW, time-bound) · **VERIFY WITH CA/LEGAL**
- **What it is:** the **technical** implementation of DPDP-aligned features in client apps:
  - consent capture and notices
  - a data-principal rights request portal
  - retention and deletion jobs
  - access logs and breach-response workflows
- **Important boundary:** TecHaust implements the software. **Legal interpretation comes from the client's lawyer or a partner law firm.** We never present our work as legal compliance certification.
- **Who buys:** Indian SMBs, fintechs, clinics, edtechs and CA firms that hold customer personal data.
- **Why demand lasts:** core obligations start 13 May 2027, with penalties up to ₹250 Cr, and the requirement continues after that (new apps, audits).
- **Skills:** ✅ engineering · ❌ legal; **partner with a data-protection lawyer** (Q-B12).
- **Deliverables:** data map, gap list (reviewed by the legal partner), implemented consent and rights features, admin screens, documentation.
- **Pricing:** fixed per app, from ₹75k.

- **Who buys:** non-technical founders, SMB owners with in-house or outsourced developers, international startups.

- **Skills:** ✅ (10+ yrs); the founder leads this ([04 §0](04-prd.md), S4).

- **Who buys:** SMB and mid-market operations teams (finance, legal, logistics, distribution), plus SaaS companies replacing per-seat tools.

#### S9 · AI Document Automation (LEAD, beachhead)
- **What it is:** extract data from invoices, purchase orders, delivery proofs, KYC files and contracts, then validate it and post it into Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero or an ERP. Includes human review screens and exception workflows. Typical uses:
  - matching vendor invoices against purchase orders and GSTR-2B
  - posting vouchers to Tally from emailed PDFs
  - extracting contract clauses
- **Who buys:** CA and accounting firms, finance teams in SMBs, NBFCs and fintechs, logistics companies, e-commerce sellers.
- **Why demand lasts:**
  - Finance and accounting is the largest document-processing use case (about 45%).
  - India's document-processing market is projected to grow about 33% a year.
  - GST e-invoicing rules create recurring reconciliation work.
  - Back-office automation showed the best ROI in MIT's study.
- **Skills:** ✅ AI/LLM · ✅ Tally integration (Tally's XML gateway and its TDL scripting language are niche; the team has the experience, [03 §0](03-plan.md)) · 🟡 finance domain knowledge (recommend a CA advisor, Q-B13).

- **Pricing:** fixed pilot from ₹2L / $8k → fixed production rollout from ₹5L / $20k (internal guide only; the site says "quoted after the pilot", [07 §4.5](07-content.md)) → AI Ops retainer. Optional per-document pricing with a monthly floor.

### 5.3 What not to lead with (and why)
- **Standalone brochure websites and generic SEO:** commoditised, and they drag down perceived seniority. Offer them only bundled with a care plan.
- **Generic "we build chatbots":** commoditised by DIY platforms.
- **Selling standalone "AI security / compliance" to regulated enterprises:** needs certifications (ISO 27001 / SOC 2) and long sales cycles. Instead, guardrails and evaluation are built into every AI build (audit findings C-2 and H-11).
- **Model training or fine-tuning:** low fit and low demand for this size of team.
