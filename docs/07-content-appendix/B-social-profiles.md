## 2. Bios: pick the longest that fits

### Short (86 characters)
For limits around 100 characters (for example the Facebook Page intro).

```text
Build it right. Automate the rest. Software & AI automation studio, India, since 2019.
```

### Medium (140 characters)
For limits of about 150 characters or more: X, Instagram, Threads, the Upwork tagline. (WhatsApp Business "About" allows 139: use the 87-character line below.)

```text
Build it right. Automate the rest.
Software & AI automation studio · India · Since 2019
Custom apps · Tally, Zoho, GST & WhatsApp automation
```

Where line breaks aren't allowed (the LinkedIn company tagline, GitHub), use one line:

```text
Build it right. Automate the rest. · Software & AI automation studio · India · Since 2019 · Custom apps · Tally, Zoho, GST & WhatsApp automation
```

If that one line is too long (LinkedIn's tagline allows about 120 characters; WhatsApp Business "About" 139), use (87 characters):

```text
Build it right. Automate the rest. · Software & AI automation studio, India, since 2019
```

### Long (about 430 characters)
For "About" or description fields: LinkedIn About, YouTube channel description, Google Business Profile description, WhatsApp Business description, Clutch, GoodFirms, Upwork overview.

```text
TecHaust Technologies is a senior software and AI automation studio in India, serving clients since 2019. We build the software your business runs on, and automate the paperwork around it: invoices into Tally, orders from WhatsApp, reports that write themselves. Fixed prices, written scope, and support after launch. We work with businesses in India and internationally.

Balurghat, West Bengal · techaust.com · contact@techaust.com
```

## 4. Rules (from [07 §1](../07-content.md))
- **"Senior"** and **"10+ years' experience"** (each core team member) and **"since 2019"** are owner-confirmed (2026-10-16, 07 §1.4). "10+ years" is the team's experience, not the company's age: never write "10+ years in business".
- No other numbers ("20+ projects") until confirmed in the claims register; no client names or logos without the client's written yes.
- Never: `100%`, `guarantee`, `trusted by`, `#1`, `best-in-class`, `world-class`, `cutting-edge`, `next-gen`, `revolutionary`, `limited slots`, `act now` (the full list is 07 §1.3).
- India/UK spelling; "&" is fine in bios, "and" in long text.
- No GSTIN, proprietor details or full address on any social profile.
