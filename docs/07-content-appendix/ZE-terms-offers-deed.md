## A. Offer wording (emails, PDF, portal)
1. Validity dates show "(IST)", e.g. "Valid until 15 Nov 2026 (IST)", on the PDF, the email and the portal; not on proformas or due dates, and not on staff screens.
2. Portal, after expiry: "This {proposal/estimate} expired at the end of {date} (IST). Reply to our email or contact us if you'd still like to go ahead."
3. From version 2 onwards (PDF under the validity note, email after the first paragraph, portal under the title): "This is version {n}. It replaces version {n−1} and any earlier version, which can no longer be accepted."
4. The email's closing line: "Questions? Just reply to this email. To accept, use Review and accept: a reply by email isn't an acceptance."
5. Staff reply, sent the same working day, if a client "accepts" by email: "Thank you, {first name}. To accept {number} v{n}, please use the Review and accept button in our email, or sign in at {portal link}, and click Accept there. As our terms say, a reply by email isn't an acceptance, so we haven't treated it as one. If it has expired or we've sent a newer version, tell us and we'll send you the current one."
6. Consumer protection: "If you are a consumer under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, nothing in these terms limits your rights under that Act, including where you may bring a complaint."

## B. Proposal terms, version 4 (items 1–5 are version 3's word for word)
7. **6. Validity and acceptance.** "This proposal or estimate is valid until the end of the date shown on it, India Standard Time (IST). You accept it only by typing your name and clicking Accept in our client portal; a reply by email, letter or message is not an acceptance. When we send a newer version, it replaces the earlier ones, which can no longer be accepted."
8. **7. Ending the work.** "Either of us may end this work with 15 days' written notice; email is fine. If you ask us to stop sooner, we stop that day. You pay for work done up to the end and for third-party costs we've already committed for you; we refund anything you've paid for work not done, as our Refunds and cancellations policy says. Care plans and subscriptions need 30 days' notice. Once you've paid everything due, the Ownership clause applies to the work delivered up to the end. Items 8 to 10 still apply after the work ends."
9. **8. Confidentiality.** "Each of us keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for this work, while the work lasts and for 3 years after it ends; trade secrets and personal data stay protected for as long as they remain confidential or the law requires. Either of us may share it with our own staff or contractors who need it and must keep it confidential, or when a law, court or regulator requires it (telling the other first where we're allowed to). Information isn't confidential if it was already public or already known to the one receiving it, came lawfully from someone else, or was developed independently."
10. Added to item 8 (approved): "We name you as a client, or show the work publicly, only with your written permission."
11. **9. Liability.** "Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or remote losses, or for lost profits or revenue. Each side's total liability under this proposal or estimate is limited to the fees you have paid us under it. These limits don't apply to fraud, to deliberate wrongdoing, to your payment of our fees, or to any liability the law doesn't allow to be limited."
12. **10. Law and disputes.** "Indian law governs this agreement. If you are based in India, the courts at Balurghat, Dakshin Dinajpur, West Bengal, have exclusive jurisdiction. If you are based outside India, any dispute we can't settle by talking within 30 days is finally decided by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and the Rules of the Mumbai Centre for International Arbitration (MCIA): one arbitrator, seated in Kolkata, in English, with hearings by video where the arbitrator allows." Line 6 (consumer rights) follows it.

## D. The deed of assignment (one page, signed by the owner alone)
17. The assignor is "Rupak Sarkar, sole proprietor, trading as TecHaust Technologies" (a proprietorship isn't a legal person). The client doesn't sign.
18. It names the work (the proposal number and Schedule 1); the price (full payment received, no royalty: Copyright Act s.19(3)); all rights, worldwide, for the full term, with no lapse if unused; what isn't assigned (the owner's pre-existing tools, under a free permanent licence, and open-source parts); that the author keeps the right to be named but won't object to the client's changes; the owner's confirmation of ownership and no other promises; Indian law, with disputes as in line 12.
19. Signed by the owner with Aadhaar eSign. West Bengal stamp duty stays OPEN (08-C RB-21).
20. Made automatically only once payments exist (M6.1); until then the owner makes it by hand. The deed never claims a lawyer has reviewed it (the owner wants a one-off review someday).
