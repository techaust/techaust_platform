# M6.3 staging fixes: a proforma's online payment counted twice; a near-blank last page on the invoice PDF

The owner approved M6.3 on staging on 2026-11-02 after two sandbox card payments. The test of the USD proforma exposed two money gaps (both there since M6.1/M6.2, not new in M6.3), and the owner's download of the export invoice showed a layout fault. The owner said "fix it first" (before M6.4 PayPal). Lead-written brief: a small fix of two guards; the reviewer's review stands in for a separate early check (lead decision, recorded in 13).

## What happened on staging (2026-11-02, read-only reads of `techaust-staging` by the lead)
1. 09:14 UTC: Stripe paid USD proforma TH/PI/2627/0002 ($23.60). As designed, the finance Worker held the payment `pending_verification` (provider `stripe`, `requested_allocations` naming the proforma, `checkout_id` set) with an open `review_items` row `advance_blocked` ("Confirm by issuing", the USD rate needed).
2. 09:20 UTC: the Owner issued the export invoice for the payment received from the proforma through the advance dialog **without** that payment (`POST /api/v1/invoices/:id/advance-invoice` with no `paymentId`): a NEW `manual` payment of $23.60 was confirmed with an advance receipt (TH/EXP/2627/0002), the proforma converted. Nothing stopped a manual receipt for a proforma that already had a gateway payment waiting for it (the `reference_seen` guard only matches the same reference, and the Owner can't know the Stripe PaymentIntent id).
3. 09:22 UTC: the Owner confirmed the waiting Stripe payment through the ordinary payment decision (`payment.verify`). `apps/admin/worker/payments.ts` (decision route, about line 736) takes `f.allocations ?? parseRequested(row.requested_allocations)`: the dialog's own allocations replace the request, so the L5 rule "money on a proforma is confirmed only by issuing the tax invoice for the payment received" (`USE_ADVANCE_INVOICE`) never saw the proforma. The Stripe payment is now `confirmed` with **no** allocation (credit held), the review item resolved as `confirmed`.
4. Result: the client shows $47.20 received for $23.60 billed; the books say a bank receipt for money that came by card. Not reachable with live money yet (`PAYMENTS_LIVE_ALLOWED` is "false"), but it must be impossible before M6.4.
5. Also seen: the proforma's note says 'A Razorpay payment on it is waiting for its tax invoice…' (`apps/admin/src/lib/payments.ts:110`) for a Stripe payment.

The staging rows stay as they are (evidence; the lead decides their clean-up after the fix).

### Why the owner's invoice had a near-blank page 2
No `break-*` rule caused it: the content didn't fit. The A4 text area ends at 279 mm (the 18 mm bottom margin holds the running footer). The owner's page 1 ended about 11 mm above that, and the integrity line needed 16 mm (a 10 mm margin, 2 mm padding, the hairline and a 7.5 pt line). Nothing kept the line with the content before it, so it went to page 2 alone. The "25 mm free above the footer" includes the footer's 18 mm margin.

### What changed (layout only)
1. **The integrity line is never alone on a page** (`styles.ts`): `break-before: avoid` on `.integrity` (proposals, estimates, every invoice-family document). The same rule is on the statement's last lines (`.stm-foot`) and the deed's closing line (`.deed__footer`).
2. **The owner's case fits on one page** (`invoice.ts`): on invoices the integrity line now sits 5 mm under the content (was 10 mm; the hairline marks the end). The signatory column's gap is 5 mm (was 8 mm), and the INR block keeps its 4 mm under the totals. The owner's case now ends at 261.4 of 263 mm.
3. **The integrity line is the last block inside the last container** (`invoice.ts`). Chromium honours `break-before: avoid` between siblings. On a continued page it ignored the rule when the line came after the money block from outside: a 24-line credit note left the line alone on page 3. The line is now the last block of the notes' text, or of the money block when there are no notes. It still spans the full 178 mm, and the notes keep their 132 mm width.
4. **The invoice totals are never split** (`invoice.ts`). `.totals { break-before: avoid }` (keep the totals with the last line) made Chromium split the totals instead: Total on one page, then the balance, the INR values and the signature overleaf. This happened on a long USD export invoice and a long USD proforma. The rule is removed, and the totals block now moves to the next page whole.
5. **The invoice notes end together** (`invoice.ts`). The last notes block stays with the one before it, and a list's last item stays with the item before it. A short notes block now moves over whole, and no page holds a single list item with the integrity line.
6. **Registration line** (`document.ts`, `statement.ts`): "GSTIN … · State …" now breaks only between its parts, and the separator is dropped at the break, as the invoice already did. The proposal left " ·" dangling at the end of a line, and the statement broke inside "State West Bengal (19)". `STATEMENT_LAYOUT_ID` is now `statement-3` (it versions the cached statement PDF; the hash already holds the IST day, so the bump only makes caches from the same day render again).
7. **Certificate page numbers** (`certificate-skeleton.ts`, `render.ts`): the accepted document's number with "Page X of Y" on the right, as on every other document. A certificate with long values runs to two pages, and page 2 didn't say what it belonged to. The words reuse the existing `PDF_COPY.page`.

Layout ids: invoices, proposals and certificates have none. Each PDF is rendered once and stored write-once in `DOCS`, so a layout change applies to new renders only. The deed's `DEED_PDF_LAYOUT_ID` (`deed-v1`) is only logged and printed nowhere, so it is left as is for one `break-before` rule (open question 3).
