# M6.3 staging fixes: a proforma's online payment counted twice; a near-blank last page on the invoice PDF

The owner approved M6.3 on staging on 2026-11-02 after two sandbox card payments. The test of the USD proforma exposed two money gaps (both there since M6.1/M6.2, not new in M6.3), and the owner's download of the export invoice showed a layout fault. The owner said "fix it first" (before M6.4 PayPal). Lead-written brief: a small fix of two guards; the reviewer's review stands in for a separate early check (lead decision, recorded in 13).

## What happened on staging (2026-11-02, read-only reads of `techaust-staging` by the lead)
1. 09:14 UTC: Stripe paid USD proforma TH/PI/2627/0002 ($23.60). As designed, the finance Worker held the payment `pending_verification` (provider `stripe`, `requested_allocations` naming the proforma, `checkout_id` set) with an open `review_items` row `advance_blocked` ("Confirm by issuing", the USD rate needed).
2. 09:20 UTC: the Owner issued the export invoice for the payment received from the proforma through the advance dialog **without** that payment (`POST /api/v1/invoices/:id/advance-invoice` with no `paymentId`): a NEW `manual` payment of $23.60 was confirmed with an advance receipt (TH/EXP/2627/0002), the proforma converted. Nothing stopped a manual receipt for a proforma that already had a gateway payment waiting for it (the `reference_seen` guard only matches the same reference, and the Owner can't know the Stripe PaymentIntent id).
3. 09:22 UTC: the Owner confirmed the waiting Stripe payment through the ordinary payment decision (`payment.verify`). `apps/admin/worker/payments.ts` (decision route, about line 736) takes `f.allocations ?? parseRequested(row.requested_allocations)`: the dialog's own allocations replace the request, so the L5 rule "money on a proforma is confirmed only by issuing the tax invoice for the payment received" (`USE_ADVANCE_INVOICE`) never saw the proforma. The Stripe payment is now `confirmed` with **no** allocation (credit held), the review item resolved as `confirmed`.
4. Result: the client shows $47.20 received for $23.60 billed; the books say a bank receipt for money that came by card. Not reachable with live money yet (`PAYMENTS_LIVE_ALLOWED` is "false"), but it must be impossible before M6.4.
5. Also seen: the proforma's note says 'A Razorpay payment on it is waiting for its tax invoice…' (`apps/admin/src/lib/payments.ts:110`) for a Stripe payment.

The staging rows stay as they are (evidence; the lead decides their clean-up after the fix).

## Brief, part 1 (builder): the two money guards (Tier A)
**PRD:** ADM-PAY-01, ADM-PAY-03, ADM-INV-10. **Rules relied on:** M6.1 L5 (a proforma's money is confirmed only by issuing the tax invoice for the payment received), RA-17 (08-C; tags as worded there).

Files (data, logic, API): `apps/admin/worker/invoice-advance.ts`, `apps/admin/worker/payments.ts` (the decision route), `packages/db/src/issue.ts` (`advanceIssueStatements`, its freeze `WHERE`), `packages/db/src/ledger.ts` if the decision batch's SQL holds the rule, the gateway paths in `apps/admin/worker/gateway/` only to read how a gateway pending payment names its proforma (`requested_allocations`, `checkout_id` → `checkout_sessions.document_id`), `apps/admin/worker/payment-messages.ts` and `@techaust/core` for codes and words.

1. **A manual advance is refused while an online payment waits for the proforma.** `POST /invoices/:id/advance-invoice` without `paymentId` answers 409 with a new code (e.g. `online_payment_waiting`) and `details.paymentId` of the waiting entry, when the proforma has a payment with `provider <> 'manual'`, status `pending_verification`, that asks for it (its `requested_allocations` names it, or its checkout is for it). `confirmDuplicate` does not pass it. The same rule sits in the freeze's `WHERE` (a webhook can land while the dialog is open), and `explain()` names it when the batch refused. Decide and report whether a waiting **manual** entry asking for the proforma should refuse the same way (it already surfaces through `reference_seen` only when the reference matches); recommend, don't widen silently.
2. **A payment that asks for a proforma is confirmed only by issuing.** The decision route judges L5 on the entry's own `requested_allocations` (and, for a gateway payment, its checkout's document) as well as on `f.allocations`: if either names a proforma (or the checkout is a proforma's), confirming answers `USE_ADVANCE_INVOICE` with `details.documentId`, whatever allocations the dialog sends. Hold the rule in the batch too if the batch can be reached otherwise. Rejecting stays as it is (a gateway payment is never rejected; M6.5 refunds).
3. **The resolve of a review item never confirms money around L5.** Check `apps/admin/worker/gateway/routes.ts` ("Mark as resolved", "Try again") for the same hole; fix the same way if found.
4. **Tests** (workerd, in the files beside each route): for Razorpay **and** Stripe pending payments on an INR and a USD proforma: the manual advance refused (read path and the freeze path: a payment recorded between the read and the batch), the confirm refused with dialog allocations empty, on an invoice, or on another document, the advance with `paymentId` still working and resolving the review item, the ordinary invoice payments unchanged.
5. Report the staging clean-up options for the two rows (which entry to withdraw, through which existing route, and what the ledger shows after), without running anything against staging.
