## Lead's check of the draft (2026-10-27)
Checked against the Cloudflare docs (docs MCP, read-only):
- **Requests:** a call through a service binding is billed as part of the first request, not as a second one (developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/#service-bindings, "only on Workers Standard pricing"). Staff traffic is far below the Free plan's 100,000 a day either way.
- **Limits:** each binding call counts toward the caller's subrequests, and one request may chain at most 32 Worker invocations (/workers/runtime-apis/bindings/service-bindings/#limits). One forward per request is fine.
- **CPU:** pricing bills "the total amount of CPU time used across both" Workers; the limits page says 10 ms "per HTTP request" without saying whether a bound Worker gets its own 10 ms. **Lead decision:** plan for the safe reading, the sum (LD7). The front's forward costs well under 1 ms, so today's work fits as before; the split buys bytes and startup room, not CPU.
- Still unverified until the builder's dry runs and the first staging deploy: the Vite auxiliary-Worker output path and deploy, the vitest pool with the binding, header and body fidelity (`CF-Connecting-IP`, `Content-Length` for uploads), per-Worker invocation logs, the `wrangler rollback` flags.
