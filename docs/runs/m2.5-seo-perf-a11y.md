**Layout (1200 × 630, light only):** stone-50 ground; the primary lockup (290 px, top left at 96/92) and the mark at 600 px in stone-200, tone-on-tone, bleeding off the top right. **Title rule (the fallback the brief asked me to name):**
1. The whole title, at the first size step that fits: 64 px cap (up to 2 lines), then 56 (2), 50 (2), 50 (3), 44 (2), 44 (3). Lines are at most 940 px wide and balanced (the smallest widest line). A break after ":" or "," is preferred, and a size whose best split leaves one short word alone on the last line is skipped.
2. Else, the part of the title before its first ": " (the title's own short form), under the same rule.
3. Else, as many whole words as fit in three lines, ending in an ellipsis.

### 2. Best Practices 96 on `/contact` and `/get-a-quote`: found and fixed (assertion back to 1)

The DevTools Issues (CDP `Audits.issueAdded`, the source of Lighthouse's `inspector-issues`) showed one `ContentSecurityPolicyIssue`: `violatedDirective: script-src`, `kEvalViolation`, from our own `/_astro/forms.*.js` at column 2015. Parse results are unchanged and only zod's compiled fast path is off; the bundle stays at 32.5 KB. I did not change `packages/core` or the CSP.
