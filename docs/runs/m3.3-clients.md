### How the hard parts work
- **Finance boundary:** one mapper `clientView(row, role)`; Sales gets no key of `CLIENT_FINANCE_FIELDS` and no `currencyLocked`; Sales gets 400 on a `gstStatus` or `currency` list filter; the activity query adds `action NOT IN (FINANCE_AUDIT_ACTIONS)` for roles without finance read.
- **Currency lock:** `UPDATE ... WHERE id AND updated_at = expected AND NOT EXISTS (invoice-family document)`; when nothing matches, one re-read picks 404, 409 `stale_version` or 409 `currency_locked`; a trigger abort also maps to `currency_locked`. No read-then-write.
- **Contacts:** audit-first for create and update (the audit row carries the conditions: client exists, address free at that client, version matches, not anonymised); the old primary is unset, then the new row set, then access revoked, all guarded by that audit row.
- **Erase and anonymise:** nothing is read before the batch. The audit row is inserted only while the contact is live, no hold on the contact, its client or a lead matched by email (the contact's client or no client), and (erase) `NOT CONTACT_ON_ISSUED_DOCUMENT_SQL`. Then, each guarded by that row: lead activity bodies to NULL, bell rows deleted, leads anonymised (name, email, phone, company, message, call window, UTM, source page, referrer, next-action note; `ipHash` removed from the consent), sessions and links deleted, an individual client's record anonymised when this was its last live contact, then the contact deleted or turned into the tombstone. When nothing is written one read picks 404, 409 `contact_anonymised`, `legal_hold` or `on_issued_document`. Kept: issued-document snapshots, acceptances, audit, access and email logs, suppressions, `auth_attempts`.
- **Export:** a step-up call returns the summary and manifest, then one part per call, up to 50 rows (keyset; the cursor holds an id or a time and an id, never a token hash). The audit row is written after the data is read and before it is returned (a failing audit write returns nothing). Raw column names (snake case) are returned; the browser assembles the file.

### Decisions I took (please confirm or change)
1. **Finance fields** are the nine listed above (tax identity and billing terms); `country` is readable by everyone but only the billing route changes it, because it decides what a GST status may be.
2. **Client and contact audit rows are all written on entity `client`** (the contact's id is in the summary), so the client's activity shows them after an erase; the summary key for a client code is `clientCode` (an existing audit test forbids a `"code"` key).
3. **`isPrimary` can only be turned on** (a client keeps a primary through another contact being promoted); `false` is 400.
4. **List filters** `gstStatus` and `currency` are applied on the keyset scan; there is no index for them (the brief's index list has none, and a new index needs your word). Fine at this size.
5. Search shows contacts by email prefix to every role with `clients.read` (including the accountant), and never an anonymised contact.
6. A lead is matched to a contact by the lower-cased email, at the contact's client or with no client. A lead with no client that two clients' contacts share is anonymised when either is erased.
7. The Sales role may create clients with their finance fields (needed to convert and to start a client) but cannot read them back; `POST /clients` answers Sales with the id, code and warnings only.
