## Designer report: preview (2026-10-22, designer, Opus 5.5 high)

Branch `feat/m2.1-site-shell`, commits `5c63e16`, `b6b3d4a`, `0d3c599`, `4c2ea3d`, `a91d491` (not pushed). Stopped after the preview, as briefed: nothing further is built until the owner approves.

### The look, in one paragraph
Flat ink and verdigris on stone, with nothing soft, 3D or "tech" about it. The header is a quiet bar: on the page colour at the top, then on the surface colour with a hairline once the page scrolls (it stays put, no hide-on-scroll). The current page is marked by a short verdigris bar with its end cut at 45°, the T bar of the mark in miniature (the 06 §4.3 signature, used once). The menus are flat panels with hairlines: Services has five columns (Advise, Build, Automate, Connect, Run), each with its group name in Archivo Expanded and its one-line intro from the services hub, plus an "All services" strip. The mobile menu is full screen, with large expanded type for the six items, two settings rows (₹/$, theme) and a full-width CTA. The footer is the shell's one large brand moment: an ink surface in both themes, with the TH mark tone-on-tone bleeding off the bottom-right corner (masked from the generated symbol file, never redrawn). The consent banner is a small white sheet (full width on phones, a card bottom-right from 768 px) with two equal buttons.

### Accessibility (scripted pass `.logs/m2.1-preview/audit.mjs`, adapted from M1.6; run in light and dark at 375, 768 and 1280 on all three pages, plus the open mobile menu)
- **Problems: none** on the final run.
  - Every control is at least 44 × 44 px (the ₹/$ segments were 42 × 40 and are now 44 × 44; the "Terms" link was 39 px wide and now has a 44 px minimum).
  - The one exception is "Preferences" in the banner: an inline text button whose hit area is extended to 44 px by `::after`. Logged as a note.
- Every control has a name.
- Exactly one h1 per page, no heading jumps.
- Landmarks: one banner, main and contentinfo; navs "Main", "Footer", "Legal" (preview specimens labelled "Main (specimen …)").
- No duplicate ids; the skip link is the first focusable element; `lang="en-IN"`; no horizontal scroll at 375 (one overflow at 375 on the components page was found and fixed).
- Text: smallest 12 px (captions, labels in frames), body 16 px.
- Lowest text contrast:
  - light: 5.33:1 (the amber "Sample" label); 5.90:1 for the eyebrow and accent text
  - dark: 7.27:1
  - mobile menu: 6.34:1 (the CTA label)
- Keyboard, all checked:
  - Tab order: skip link → banner (Preferences, Reject, Accept) → logo → Services → … (the banner comes early in the focus order on purpose).
  - Enter or Space opens a menu; Tab moves into the panel; Esc closes it and focus returns to the trigger (`aria-expanded` goes back to false).
  - In the mobile menu focus starts on Close and Tab cycles inside the dialog (it wrapped from the CTA back to the logo, never leaving the dialog); Esc closes it and focus returns to "Menu".
  - Focus rings are visible in both themes (screenshots `kbd-*`).
- Reduced motion: every transition duration computes to 0 s, and there are no running animations (`document.getAnimations()` is empty). Nothing loops.
- Console: no errors or warnings on the preview pages (the only errors in the session were a hot-reload glitch while editing, the browser's automatic `/favicon.ico` request (404), and links to pages that don't exist yet).

### Deviations from the docs (each is a question below where it's the owner's)
1. **Full nav from 1280 px, not 1024 px.**
   - Measured: at 1024 px the six items, the two toggles and the CTA need about 1,150 px and the bar has about 950, so they overlapped.
   - Below 1280 px the header is logo + CTA (from 768 px) + "Menu" (the full-screen menu, which holds the toggles).
   - 06 §4.2 says "lg 1024: full nav with dropdowns" and "md: inline nav for ≤ 5 items + More".
2. **Header CTA target:** `/contact?service=discovery-sprint`, as the brief says, while 07 §2.3 says "Book a Discovery Sprint" goes to `/get-a-quote?service=S1`.
3. **Consent banner on wider screens:** a card bottom-right (on phones it is the full-width bottom sheet of 06 §5.2). Bottom-left covered the hero CTA at 1280 × 800; content and CTAs are left-aligned.
4. **Footer:** ink in both themes, with the tone-on-tone mark (06 doesn't specify the footer's surface).
5. **Footer "6 headline services":** read as the six home-page cards of 07 §3, plus "All services".
6. **Services menu shows the group intros of 07 §5.1** under each group name (not listed in 07 §2.1).
7. **Currency default:** INR when nothing is stored. The `/api/geo` default is phase 2.
8. **Mobile menu sections:** Services and Industries inside the mobile dialog are `<details>` (native expanded state), not scripted buttons.
9. **Rupee font:** `archivo-rupee.woff2` (1.5 KB) loads on every page, because the header's ₹ toggle is on every page (06 §3.1: "only on pages with ₹").
10. **PriceTag** strips a zero ".00" from the `formatMoney` output (string only, no maths): site prices are whole rupees and dollars.
