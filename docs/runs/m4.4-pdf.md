## Lead decisions (from the analyst's draft; docs/13)
- **L1.** Two messages per document: snapshot, then render, rendered only from the R2 envelope. The lead updates docs/05 §8.
- **L2.** Pacing:
  - wait inside the consumer so requests are at least 10 s apart (`max_concurrency 1`);
  - on a 429, send a delayed copy and acknowledge the original (rate: `Retry-After`; quota: the next UTC midnight + 120 s). No counted retries.
- **L3.** `browser` binding in staging only; a local stub renderer refused outside `ENVIRONMENT=local`; no `remote: true`; no `dev:pdf-remote` in M4.4.
- **L4.** The envelope:
  - holds `v`, `documentId`, `type`, `version`, `number`, `numberDisplay`, `series`, `fy`, `sentAt`, `snapshotSha256` and `snapshot {v: 2, header, computedJson, lines, sections, schedule, terms {id, version, sha256, bodyMd}}`;
  - is serialised with `JSON.stringify` in a fixed key order (not `canonicalJson`).
- **L5.** R2 keys:
  - fixed: `docs/<type>/<fy>/<number padded to 4>-v<version>.{json,pdf}`;
  - `If-None-Match: *` on every put, and a `head` before any re-render. A PDF is never rendered twice.
- **L6.** No `documents` column or trigger change (the PDF columns are already set-once after freeze). One index migration, regenerated after M4.3's 0031/0032 as **0033**.
- **L7.** Signature upload in M4.4 (decided 2026-10-25). No logo upload: logos come only from `BRAND_LOGOS`. M4.3 freezes `issuer.company.logo` at send.
- **L8.** Visual baselines committed for Linux only, from a CI artifact (`win32/` git-ignored).
- **L9.** M4.4 renders proposals and estimates only; other types are acknowledged as `unsupported_type`.
- **L10.** Production follow-up (Phase 7): refuse to send while the company legal name, GSTIN or address is empty when `ENVIRONMENT=production`. On staging the PDF leaves the missing fields out.
- **L11.** The dev tools are reused, per the owner's answer.
- **L12** (lead, this session). To keep two parallel builders off the same files, **phase 1 touches no file M4.3 is changing**:
  - no `apps/jobs/src/{queue,index}.ts`, no `apps/jobs/wrangler.jsonc`, no `packages/db/src/schema/documents.ts`;
  - the queue routing, the wrangler config, the index migration and the budget entry move to phase 2;
  - the one exception is an additive export line in `packages/core/package.json`, which the lead merges.

    | { v: 1; kind: "pdf.snapshot"; documentId: string; reason: "send" | "retry" | "sweep" }

**3. Render client and pacer** (`apps/jobs/src/browser.ts`, pure `apps/jobs/src/pacer.ts`; new files, not wired into the queue until phase 2).
- **`renderPdf(env, html, options)`:**
  - calls `env.BROWSER.quickAction("pdf", {html, pdfOptions, gotoOptions, setJavaScriptEnabled: false, cacheTTL: 0})`;
  - returns `{bytes, browserMs}` or a classified error:
    - `rate` (429 without "time limit"; `retryAfterS`, default 10)
    - `quota` (429 with "time limit exceeded")
    - `render` (422)
    - `upstream` (5xx, network, throw)
    - `not_pdf` (no `%PDF-` magic, wrong content type, or > 10 MB)
  - handles both a thrown error with `.status` and a returned Response.
- **`pdfOptions`:**
  - `format: "a4"`, `preferCSSPageSize: true`, `printBackground: true`, `tagged: true`, `outline: true`, `timeout: 30000`
  - `displayHeaderFooter: false` (`footerTemplate` is the fallback if staging shows page-margin boxes unsupported)
  - `gotoOptions {waitUntil: "load"}`
- **Pacer:** after every call, wait until `PDF_MIN_SPACING_MS` (default 10000) has passed since the call started (`scheduler.wait`, else a `setTimeout` promise; check workerd). Tested on a fake clock.
- **Local stub:** `PDF_RENDERER = "stub"` returns a fixed minimal one-page PDF ("Local stub: not a rendered document"). It is refused unless `ENVIRONMENT === "local"` (test).
