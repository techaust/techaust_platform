2. **`payments`**, append-only apart from the status moves below:

     - `note`: ≤ 500.
   - **Who and when:** `recorded_by`, `recorded_at`, `verified_by`, `verified_at`, `decision_note`, `withdrawn_by`, `withdrawn_at`, `withdraw_reason`.
   - **No CHECK on the enumerations** (`kind`, `provider`, `status`, `mode`). Triggers enforce them, so M6.2–M6.5 can widen them without a rebuild.
   - **Indexes:**
     - `(client_id, currency, received_on, id)`;
     - pending `(recorded_at, id) WHERE status='pending_verification'`;
     - confirmed `(received_on, id) WHERE status='confirmed'`;
     - `(reference_norm) WHERE reference_norm IS NOT NULL`.
3. **`payment_allocations`**, append-only:
   - columns: `id`, `payment_id` → payments, `document_id` → documents, `amount_minor` CHECK > 0, `kind` (`allocate` or `release`, by trigger), `created_by`, `created_at`;
   - indexes `(payment_id)` and `(document_id, payment_id)`;
   - allocations exist only for confirmed payments.
4. **`realisation_extensions`**, append-only (Q60):
   - columns: `id`, `document_id` → an export invoice, `extended_to`, `bank_ref` (≤ 64), `letter_r2_key`, `letter_sha256` and `letter_mime` (set once), `recorded_by`, `created_at`;
   - index `(document_id, extended_to)`.
5. **Triggers** (0057). Each abort message starts with the trigger's name.
   - **`payments`:**
     - inserted as `pending_verification`, or as `confirmed` with `verified_by = recorded_by`;
     - `pending_verification` moves once, to `confirmed` or `rejected`; `confirmed` moves once, to `withdrawn`;
     - the proof, TDS-certificate and withdraw columns are set once; everything else is frozen; no DELETE;
     - the enumeration checks;
     - a TDS row needs a section and a rate (1–10000 bp), has no mode, and its client is in India.
   - **`payment_allocations`:** append-only, with a BEFORE INSERT bound check:
     - the payment is confirmed, of the same client and currency;
     - the document is a frozen, not void `tax_invoice`, `export_invoice` or `debit_note`, never a proforma or a credit note;
     - `allocate`: the amount is ≤ the document's `balance_minor`, and the payment's Σ(allocate − release) plus the new amount is ≤ its amount;
     - `release`: the amount is ≤ the document's excess (paid + credited + tds − total) and ≤ what this payment holds on it;
     - a `tds` payment allocates only to an INR `tax_invoice` or `debit_note`.
   - **The post-condition view `ledger_result`** (INSTEAD OF INSERT, the `issue_result` pattern, not guarded on the audit row). It aborts unless:
     - a TDS payment is allocated exactly once and in full;
     - a payment's net allocations are ≤ its amount;
     - every document touched has paid, TDS and balance equal to the recompute.
   - **`documents_ledger_identity`** is recreated (drop, then create): `tds_minor ≥ 0`, and `balance = max(0, total − paid − credited − tds)`. The same document types are covered.
   - **New `documents_tds_bounds`:** `tds_minor > 0` only on an INR `tax_invoice` or `debit_note`.
   - **New `documents_void_no_payment`:** void is refused when `tds_minor > 0`, when an allocation exists, or when a non-rejected payment asks for this document.
   - **`approvals`:**
     - a partial unique index `approvals_payment_pending (payment_id) WHERE status='pending' AND kind='payment_verify'`;
     - a trigger: a `payment_verify` row names an existing pending payment and has `document_id` NULL.
   - **Back-fill** (in the same set, idempotent): for each `advance_receipts` row, one confirmed payment and one `allocate` row, with ids derived from the receipt's id and `advance_receipt_id` set. A test proves the recompute leaves each advance invoice's stored `paid_minor` unchanged.
6. **The ledger recompute** (`packages/db/src/ledger.ts`, new): one statement over a JSON list of document ids, never an increment.
   - `paid` = Σ allocate − Σ release over confirmed `payment` rows;
   - `tds` = Σ allocate over confirmed `tds` rows;
   - `balance = max(0, total − paid − credited − tds)`;
   - status from the CASE twin of core `ledgerStatusOf(total, paid + tds, credited)`;
   - `updated_at = max(now, updated_at + 1)`.
   - **Every existing ledger writer in `packages/db` is updated:**
     - the credit recompute (`issue.ts` about line 571) subtracts `tds_minor`;
     - the advance batch (`issue.ts` about lines 762–784) inserts the payment and its allocation and uses the recompute. The proforma's paid amount stays as M5.2 writes it.
   - The admin writers (`invoice-issue.ts` about line 472, `note-view.ts` about line 150, the void statement in `invoices.ts`) are stage B2.
7. **Proofs and bank letters go in DOCS, write-once:** keys `payments/<id>/proof-<sha256>` and `extensions/<id>/letter-<sha256>`; PDF, JPEG or PNG, at most 5 MB, sniffed as `files.ts` does.

**`POST /payments` (record):**
- The body:
  - `id`, `clientId`, `kind`, `currency`, `amountMinor`;
  - `receivedOn` (≤ today, IST), `mode`, `reference`, `chargesMinor`, `inrReceivedMinor?`;
  - `allocations` (0 to 10), the TDS fields, `note`, `confirmDuplicate?`.
- **Owner:** the payment is confirmed in the same batch, with its allocations, the recompute and `ledger_result`.
- **Staff:** the payment is pending with `requested_allocations`, plus an `approvals` row (`payment_verify`, `content_sha256`) and an `INSERT OR IGNORE` bell for each active owner. The balance doesn't move.
- **Refusals:**
  - the same `id` again: 409 `already_recorded`, nothing written;
  - a reference already seen: 409 `reference_seen` (with its date and client) unless `confirmDuplicate` is sent;
  - a requested allocation to a proforma is allowed only while the payment is pending.

**`POST /payments/:id/verify`:**
- Body: `decision`, `note`, optional `allocations`, `expectedRecordedAt`.
- Confirm uses `WHERE status='pending_verification'` plus the owner's authority in SQL. It writes the allocations, recomputes, sets the approval to approved and rings the requester's bell.
- A proforma target: 409 `use_advance_invoice`. The advance dialog, prefilled from the payment, confirms it instead: the advance batch flips the pending payment and allocates it to the new invoice, under every M5.2 rule.
- Already decided: 409 `payment_not_pending`.
- `POST /approvals/:id/decide` on a `payment_verify` row: 409 `use_payment_verify`. The approvals list carries payment items.

**Other routes:**
- **Withdraw:** a reason is required; it recomputes every document the payment touched.
- **Credit:**
