### Lead decisions for this build (recorded in docs/13)
1. **Client codes:** `C` + a 4-digit sequence (`C0001`), the next free number taken in the conversion batch; the existing `clients_code` unique index guards a race (retry once on conflict). The sample clients keep `SAMPLE-A` / `SAMPLE-B`.
2. **New lead columns by `ALTER TABLE leads ADD COLUMN` only** (`leads` is referenced by `lead_activities`, so it is never rebuilt): `duplicate_of` (the earlier lead with the same email within 30 days) and `proposal_id` (the draft made at conversion). Anything beyond `ADD COLUMN`, a new table, index or trigger: stop and report.
3. **Stage automation:** "Proposal sent" and "Won" follow the linked proposal once proposals can be sent and accepted (M4.2/M4.3). M3.2 builds and tests the one function those milestones call (proposal sent → `proposal_sent`; accepted → `won`, unless a person has set a later manual stage), and the manual moves with their rules now.
4. **Retention clean-up:** a daily cron at 03:30 IST (22:00 UTC) in the jobs Worker that **only enqueues** (JOB-CRON-01), one lead per message on the **existing** `LEADS_QUEUE` as a new message kind (no new queue, no hosted change). A lead is due when it is not converted (`client_id` null), has no legal hold and `delete_after` has passed; `delete_after` = last activity + `retention.leadsMonths`, recomputed on every activity. The consumer re-checks those conditions before deleting. **A dry-run mode is on everywhere until the owner says otherwise** (a `LEAD_PURGE_MODE` var, `dry_run` locally and on staging, no production value until Phase 7): it writes what it would delete to the log (lead ref only) and deletes nothing.
5. **Reply from admin (ADM-LEAD-06):** through `EMAIL_QUEUE` and `email_log` like every other email, from `hello@` with Reply-To `hello@`; free text is sent as plain text and escaped in HTML. The ready-made templates come from 07-I, drafted by the lead for the owner's word-for-word approval; until then only free text is offered and the template list is empty.

**Clean-up job (`apps/jobs/src/purge.ts`, `queue.ts`, `index.ts`, `wrangler.jsonc`):**
