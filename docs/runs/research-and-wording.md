### Contract changes (builder, 2026-10-28; reasons given)
- **Advance `route: "lut"` on a proforma that charged IGST** is 400 `bad_request` (a payment cannot move from tax charged to none). `route` for a domestic client is 400 `bad_request`. `advance_lut_missing` answers 409 with `error.details = { fy: "2027-28" }` (the FY label, not the token); `err()` now takes optional `details`.
- **FX (RA-21):** on the advance route the rate is judged at once: 400 `fx_same_day_exists` (an earlier day without `noRateThatDay: true`, however old) or 400 `fx_date_invalid` (later day, or with the flag more than `fxMaxAgeDays` older). On a **draft** (PATCH `fields.fx`) nothing is judged at save (the day of supply is the issue day, unknown then): the same two codes come as **blockers** (409 `issue_blocked`, `blockers: [{code, date}]`; new blocker code `fx_same_day_exists`). The invoice view's `fx.value` gains `noRateThatDay`, and `fx` gains `dateVerdict` (`ok | same_day_exists | invalid | null`).
