## Brief
**IDs:** 08-C RB-38(a) (Researched 2026-10-31, Copyright Act s.19(1); IT Act s.5 unverified), RB-21 (OPEN), ADM-PRJ-01, ADM-PRJ-04, POR-05; copy [07-ZE](../07-content-appendix/ZE-terms-offers-deed.md) section D and "The deed's text" (approved word for word; the approved lines win over this brief).

**Owner's decisions (13, 2026-10-31):**
- The deed is **prepared automatically** when the accepted proposal or estimate behind a project is **paid in full and the project is marked Completed**. The Owner gets a bell; staff fill Schedule 1 (the work) and Schedule 2 (not assigned).
- The owner signs **by hand on an Aadhaar eSign provider's website** and uploads the signed PDF; nothing hosted is built for eSign (07-ZE Q4).
- After the upload, the signed deed appears in the client's **portal under that proposal or estimate**, and the client's portal contacts get a **short email** (new copy, 07-ZJ, for the owner's approval).
- The footer is the fixed line **"Stamp duty: Not stamped at signing. Any stamp duty due will be paid before this deed is produced as evidence."** (owner-approved; tag `OPEN (08-C RB-21)`).
- The assignor is "Rupak Sarkar, sole proprietor, trading as TecHaust Technologies"; the client doesn't sign; the deed never claims a lawyer reviewed it.

**Lead decisions (record in 13 at integration):**
- **L1 Number:** a new gap-free series `deed` in `packages/core/src/numbering.ts` `SERIES`, label "Deed of assignment", `gst: false`, default `TH/DA/{FY}/{N}` (15 characters at padding 4), assigned when the deed is finalised, inside one `db.batch()`, like every other number.
- **L2 Full payment** (pure core function, twin-tested against its SQL): the project has `source_document_id` = an **accepted** proposal or estimate version; the project's live invoice-family documents (tax and export invoices, tax invoices for a payment received, debit notes; never proformas, never voided) all have `balance_minor = 0`; at least one exists; and the money received against them (from the M6.1 ledger: confirmed `payments` allocations plus TDS by certificate, net of credit notes) reaches the accepted version's total in its currency. Count an advance's tax invoice and the final invoice it was adjusted into **once** (check how M5.2/M6.1 link them; the early check looks at exactly this). Care-plan projects and anonymised clients never get a deed.
- **L3 Where it's evaluated:** one SQL helper `deedDraftStatement(db, {projectId, now})` in `packages/db/src/deeds.ts` — an `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE <eligible> ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (unique per project) plus the Owner's bell guarded on it — appended to (a) the batch that sets a project to `completed` (admin API, `projects.ts`) and (b) every M6.1 ledger batch that can bring a project's invoices to zero (payment record/confirm, use credit, TDS, credit-note issue) in the finance Worker. No cron, no jobs work for detection. A "Check again" action for the Owner on the project page runs the same statement (for projects completed before T11).
- **L4 States:** `draft` (created by L3; Schedules editable by Owner and Staff; the parties, work, price and invoice numbers are recomputed on read) → `final` (Owner with step-up: number assigned, all `{…}` values frozen into a sealed snapshot with its SHA-256, the unsigned PDF rendered write-once) → `signed` (Owner with step-up uploads the signed PDF: PDF signature sniffed, ≤ 10 MB, write-once in `DOCS`, never the file vault; the owner types the eSign timestamp and transaction ID shown by the provider; then the portal row and the email). A `draft` can be **cancelled** by the Owner (reason required); `final` and `signed` are never edited or deleted (triggers, like the invoice family). If a `final` deed's project stops being fully paid (a credit note), the screen warns; nothing changes on its own.
- **L5 Signature block:** the unsigned PDF prints 07-ZE's sentence up to "TecHaust Technologies" and leaves the signature space empty; `{esign_timestamp_ist} · {esign_transaction_id}` are shown from the stored values on the admin and portal screens next to the signed copy (the provider's own signature appearance carries them inside the PDF). Record this reading of "the PDF leaves the space".
- **L6 Parties and values:** supplier name, address and GSTIN from Settings › Company at finalising; the client's legal name, address and GSTIN/registration from the client record; `{doc_type} {doc_number} v{version}` and `{accepted_date_ist}` from the accepted version; `{invoice_numbers}` and `{amount_paid}` (formatted with core money, en-IN or en-US by currency) from L2's set. Schedule 1: deliverables list, repository or delivery location, delivered dates; Schedule 2: pre-existing tools, third-party and open-source parts with licence names (Schedule 2 may be "None" but must be filled in explicitly before finalising).
- **L7 Erasure:** anonymising a client deletes its `draft` deeds; `final` and `signed` deeds are kept like invoices (a legal record of a transfer of rights); the admin and portal show "Anonymised client" by the existing helpers. Flag for the early check.
- **L8 Permissions** (`packages/core/src/permissions.ts`, docs/04 §9): `deeds.view` Owner, Staff, Accountant; `deeds.editSchedules` Owner, Staff; `deeds.finalise`, `deeds.uploadSigned`, `deeds.cancel`, `deeds.recheck` Owner (finalise and upload with step-up). Audit actions `deed.draft`, `deed.edit`, `deed.finalise`, `deed.sign`, `deed.cancel`.
