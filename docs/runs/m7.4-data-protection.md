### L30: the mechanism (for early check 1 to confirm)
1. A receipt's subject is its `subject_hash` once known; until then its `lead_id` / `contact_id`.
2. Every end writes one `ended` receipt carrying the hash: the erasure triggers (`*_erased_receipts`, source `erasure`, 0 statements; only when the row had receipts) for the contact erase, the anonymise and the lead erase; the routine purge through `PURGE_RECEIPTS_SQL` (source `retention`, +2 statements before its DELETE, guarded like it; B2 wires it into `apps/jobs/src/purge.ts`).
3. **Due** (consent family): a receipt older than the cut-off whose subject has an `ended` receipt **and** no receipt at or after the cut-off; the `erased` stop row goes when no receipt of its hash is at or after the cut-off (L29). A receipt still linked to a live lead or contact with no `ended` row is never due, however old (the proof is kept while the consent is relied on). A receipt with no hash and no link (a lead purged before hashes existed) goes on its own age.
4. The trigger floor holds it again: no receipt is deleted while it, or any receipt of the same hash, lead or contact, is under 366 days.
Proven in `packages/db/test/retention.test.ts` (an ended subject 4 years old goes with its stop; one with a 30-day-old `ended` receipt and its stop stay; a live lead's 4-year-old grant stays).

### Engineering decisions taken (for 13, "Lead" rows, or for the lead to overturn)
1. **No delete guard on `email_suppressions`.** M7.2's Owner "Allow emails again" and L20's lift delete stop rows of any age, so a floor trigger would break both; the `erased` rows' end is the consent family's predicate only.
2. **The request clock is written by every insert, never filled by a trigger.** D1's `meta.changes` counts the rows a trigger writes: an AFTER INSERT fill made the portal's `changes === 1` check fail (409). **B2 note:** the same holds for every new writing trigger: a files DELETE (outbox), a `legal_holds` INSERT (mirror), an `erased_hash` UPDATE (stop and receipts) and a redaction UPDATE (outbox) report more than one change; routes must test `> 0` or read back, never `=== 1`.
3. `hook_failures` is in the `counters` family (a 2-day window counter, not a payment record).
4. Staff tables (`staff_users`, `staff_invites`, `staff_known_devices`, `staff_recovery_codes`, `staff_notification_prefs`) are `staff_account`: kept while the account exists. L36's `staff_known_devices` row has no age rule yet (see question 3). `staff_signin_pending` keeps its M7.2 sweep; `pdf_failures` and the M6.4 advice tables are never deleted.
5. The `consent_receipts` delete floor is per person (no receipt of the same hash, lead or contact under 366 days), stronger than the brief's per-row floor.
6. A legal-hold history row needs an active Owner as `by` (the `care_auto_issue_by` precedent). There is no trigger forcing the flags to come only from the history (many existing tests set `legal_hold` directly); the route is the only writer.
7. `consent_receipts.notice_version` is NULL-able: a staff-recorded contact consent has no notice version.
8. `retention_live` (A.8) is not seeded: a missing row reads as off (C.7 writes it).
9. `email_log`, `email_suppressions` (erased), `client_requests` (closed), `pdf_asks` and `hook_failures` are scanned without a new index where the brief listed none (small tables, or an existing index serves the range); recorded for J.
