**Screens:**
1. **Portal shell** (`/preview/shell`): the header with the logo files (never the wordmark in a live font), the client's name and the signed-in contact, and a "Sign out" menu with "Sign out of all devices". Navigation: Home, Proposals and estimates, Invoices, Projects, Profile. The later sections (invoices M5–M7, projects and profile M7.1) show a calm "coming soon" state. Light and dark.
2. **Home** (`/preview/home`): a card for each open proposal or estimate awaiting a decision (title, number, version, total, valid until, a "View" action). An empty state. Project and invoice cards appear as "coming soon" placeholders only.
3. **Proposal view** (`/preview/proposal`): the HTML view of the latest sent version. It follows the 06 §7 proposal layout:
   - cover;
   - summary;
   - problem and goals;
   - scope and deliverables;
   - timeline and milestones;
   - line items with the tax summary by SAC and rate, worked out with the M4.2 engine;
   - the payment schedule;
   - terms;
   - the validity line and "Download PDF".

   Long content scrolls in one column on phones. INR and USD samples. A status pill: Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Revised.
4. **Accept** (on the proposal view):
   - **Accept panel:** the client types their full name, ticks "I have read and agree to the terms" (with a link to the terms section) and presses "Accept". There is a confirmation step, and focus moves to the result.
   - **Accepted state:** locked, with the acceptance details (name, date and time in IST) and "Download the accepted PDF" plus "Download the acceptance certificate".
5. **Decline:** a reason (required, with a short list plus "Other"), then a confirmation and the declined state.
6. **Edge states:**
   - an expired version ("valid until" has passed);
   - a superseded version (a newer one was sent: the latest is shown, the old one isn't);
   - an already accepted version (no second acceptance);
   - a document of another client (the 404 screen);
   - the single-document token view: a document opened from the email link without full sign-in. It shows only that document and an offer to sign in for the rest.
7. **Email preview** (`/preview/email`): the proposal email to the client (sent from `hello@`): greeting, the document title and number, total and validity, the "View the proposal" button (the portal link) and, when attached, the PDF note. It follows the M2.4 and M2.4b email templates in `packages/email`, light and dark. Also the acceptance confirmation email.
8. **Admin side** (`/preview/send` in `apps/admin` dev routes, or a static screenshot set): the "Send" dialog on a proposal. It shows:
   - the contacts to send to (portal-access contacts, with billing contacts pre-ticked);
   - the attach-PDF switch, which follows the client's setting;
   - the message preview;
   - "Send".

   After acceptance, the "Start project" action and its confirmation.

### Deviations from docs/06
1. **Shell:** a top band with tabs instead of 06 §5.3's side-nav AppShell: the portal has five sections and non-technical readers (06 §4.2 allows inline nav for up to 5 items from md; below md, the full-screen menu).
2. **No Radix** (06 §5.3): native `<dialog>` and a hand-built disclosure, as M3.1 (no new packages).
3. **"Declined"** for the client (the brief's word) where 06 §5.5 says "Rejected".
4. **Tax summary:** CGST and SGST show amounts, not "(rate, amount)": the engine gives no split rate and the screen must not compute one.
5. **Phone cards:** Qty and Unit share one line ("2 months") and "#" is left out; the table keeps all of 06 §7.3's columns.
6. **Not on the HTML view:** the supplier block of 06 §7.2 (GSTIN, proprietor) and §7.4's "Team" and "How we work" sections (ADM-PROP-02 lets sections be hidden; the brief's list leaves them out).
7. **Portal footer** reuses the 07 §8 email footer line.
