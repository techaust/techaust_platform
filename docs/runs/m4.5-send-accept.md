## Phase 1: design preview (designer, Opus 5.5 high)
**Goal:** the owner sees and approves the look and behaviour of the client portal before it is built for real. The full portal (M7.1) copies it.

**Branch:** `feat/m4.5-preview` from `origin/feat/m4.5-send-accept`, own worktree; push only that branch.

**First step:** load `frontend-design:frontend-design` (mandatory) and any other fitting skill (docs/14 §9), and list them. Read:
- CLAUDE.md
- docs/04 POR-01…11 and ADM-PROP-05…10
- docs/06, all of it: tokens, §5.3 admin and portal components, §5.5 document status colours, §7 the document layouts (the HTML proposal view must match the PDF layout of M4.4)
- docs/07 (the voice and the banned phrases)
- the M1.6 portal as built: `apps/portal/src`, the sign-in, callback and home routes, `app.css`, `copy.ts`
- the admin shell and the M3.x screens in `apps/admin/src`, for the shared look

**Where:**
- Real React components in `apps/portal/src`, so an approved preview becomes the start of the build.
- Shown on **dev-only** routes `/preview/*` that are never in the production build. Prove it: the built bundle has no preview route and no sample data.
- **Sample data only.** Every screen is visibly labelled "Preview — sample data". Use made-up names on `example.test` addresses and a clearly marked sample GSTIN. No API calls; nothing is saved.
- Copy that doesn't exist yet is a visible "[Placeholder]" line, listed in the report.

**Screens:**
1. **Portal shell** (`/preview/shell`): the header with the logo files (never the wordmark in a live font), the client's name and the signed-in contact, and a "Sign out" menu with "Sign out of all devices". Navigation: Home, Proposals and estimates, Invoices, Projects, Profile. The later sections (invoices M5–M7, projects and profile M7.1) show a calm "coming soon" state. Light and dark.
2. **Home** (`/preview/home`): a card for each open proposal or estimate awaiting a decision (title, number, version, total, valid until, a "View" action). An empty state. Project and invoice cards appear as "coming soon" placeholders only.
3. **Proposal view** (`/preview/proposal`): the HTML view of the latest sent version. It follows the 06 §7 proposal layout:
   - cover;
   - summary;
   - problem and goals;
   - scope and deliverables;
   - timeline and milestones;
   - line items with the tax summary by SAC and rate, worked out with the M4.2 engine;
   - the payment schedule;
   - terms;
   - the validity line and "Download PDF".

   Long content scrolls in one column on phones. INR and USD samples. A status pill: Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Revised.
4. **Accept** (on the proposal view):
   - **Accept panel:** the client types their full name, ticks "I have read and agree to the terms" (with a link to the terms section) and presses "Accept". There is a confirmation step, and focus moves to the result.
   - **Accepted state:** locked, with the acceptance details (name, date and time in IST) and "Download the accepted PDF" plus "Download the acceptance certificate".
5. **Decline:** a reason (required, with a short list plus "Other"), then a confirmation and the declined state.
6. **Edge states:**
   - an expired version ("valid until" has passed);
   - a superseded version (a newer one was sent: the latest is shown, the old one isn't);
   - an already accepted version (no second acceptance);
   - a document of another client (the 404 screen);
   - the single-document token view: a document opened from the email link without full sign-in. It shows only that document and an offer to sign in for the rest.
7. **Email preview** (`/preview/email`): the proposal email to the client (sent from `hello@`): greeting, the document title and number, total and validity, the "View the proposal" button (the portal link) and, when attached, the PDF note. It follows the M2.4 and M2.4b email templates in `packages/email`, light and dark. Also the acceptance confirmation email.
8. **Admin side** (`/preview/send` in `apps/admin` dev routes, or a static screenshot set): the "Send" dialog on a proposal. It shows:
   - the contacts to send to (portal-access contacts, with billing contacts pre-ticked);
   - the attach-PDF switch, which follows the client's setting;
   - the message preview;
   - "Send".

   After acceptance, the "Start project" action and its confirmation.

**Rules:**
- **Visual:**
  - tokens only from `packages/ui`;
  - the logo files, never hand-drawn;
  - arrows as icons;
  - plain CSS with the token custom properties, like the existing `app.css`;
  - no new packages (stop and report if one seems needed);
  - the owner's taste: flat ink and verdigris brand geometry, calm, trustworthy and easy for a non-technical client. Generic soft, 3D or stock-dashboard looks are rejected.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA; 44 px targets; text ≥ 12 px and body ≥ 16 px; visible focus; reduced motion; the keyboard works everywhere and focus returns after dialogs.
- **Language and formats:** English (India/UK spelling); no banned phrases (07 §1.3); money via `@techaust/core` formatting; dates in IST; VERIFY WITH CA/LEGAL notes where the acceptance record or the tax lines are concerned.

**Screenshots** (`.logs/m4.5-preview/`): each preview screen at 375, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark. Also: the accept confirmation step, the accepted state, decline, expired, superseded, the token view, both emails and the admin send dialog.

**Checks:** all via `bash tools/heavy.sh --log …`:
- `pnpm check`;
- `pnpm --filter @techaust/portal build`, with proof that `/preview` and the sample data are not in `dist`, and the portal budget line;
- a scripted a11y pass: axe on each screen in both themes, 44 px, names, focus.

**Stop after the preview:**
1. Commit and push `feat/m4.5-preview`.
2. Append "Designer report: preview" here: screens, components, the screenshot list, a11y results, the budget line, every placeholder, deviations from 06, and open questions.
3. Stop every dev server.
4. Stop. **Nothing further is built until the owner approves** (a decision row in docs/13).
