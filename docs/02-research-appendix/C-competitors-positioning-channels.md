### Common patterns [I, built on the sourced rows above]
1. **Fixed price after discovery is now standard.** Hourly is mostly a directory label. Almost every small competitor sells fixed-scope sprints with milestone billing (30/40/30 or 50/50) and 30–90 days of included support.
2. **Published price anchors are common among small players** (Innovatrix, Xenotix, HouseofMVPs, Ailoitte, XRAY). Larger firms hide pricing.
3. **The "AI-first / AI-native" label is saturated.** Nearly every Indian firm now says it, from 3-person shops to 18,000-person ones. It no longer differentiates.
4. **Proof is mostly self-reported counts** ("110+ products", "97% retention") and logos. Few small firms show named, metric-backed case studies. Those that do (Innovatrix, Xenotix) stand out.
5. **Offshore-objection handling is explicit:** IP transfer, overlap hours, a registered entity (LLPIN), a US address, "no rotating bench".
6. **India-based SMB automation is converging on WhatsApp + n8n + Tally/Zoho/Shopify.**

### White space [I]
- **A senior, founder-delivered studio that combines product engineering with ops automation.** MVP shops don't do back-office automation, and automation shops (n8n/Make) don't build durable products. SingleBit has the senior-founder angle but no automation or vertical focus. Innovatrix has automation but sells on low price and D2C.
- **Finance, accounting and legal ops automation for SMBs and accounting firms, delivered to India *and* abroad.** Satva owns accounting *integrations* (a .NET/API shop). Nobody small clearly owns "AI document and reconciliation automation for CA/CPA firms and finance teams" across Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero. Well-funded vertical SaaS exists (Botkeeper, Docyt, Puzzle; Accrual raised $75M) **[S]** (https://puzzle.io/blog/ai-native-bookkeeping-cpa-firms, https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/a-big-year-for-ai-in-accounting). So the opening is to integrate and customise around these tools, not to compete with them.
- **Transparent productised retainers at Indian cost for international SMBs.** Western dev subscriptions start at about $1,000–3,000/mo. An Indian senior team could credibly offer **$1,200–2,500/mo** (about ₹1–2.2L) with an SLA, which is still under Western floors and above Indian commodity rates.
- **AMC done well.** TecHaust already has AMC and retainer clients. Few competitors package maintenance, monitoring and "next automation" as a named product with a published SLA.

**Recommended hybrid [I]:** *"A senior product and automation studio for finance-heavy SMBs, accounting firms and SaaS teams."* Lead with option **B** (finance, accounting and legal ops automation) as the beachhead. Package the delivery as **C** (fixed-price pilot → productised retainer/AMC). Keep **A/D** as the "we also build the product" offering for SaaS and startup clients. Keep the Corporate Brain / Digital Workforce / System Guard names as **service lines, or as future products**, not as an enterprise-infrastructure claim. This fits the existing industries (finance/legal, retail/logistics, SaaS) and the productised-agency → products path. Repeated vertical workflows (for example GST/2B reconciliation or invoice extraction to Tally/Zoho) can later become SaaS.

### How small Indian studios build trust internationally
- **Reviews:** 94% of B2B buyers use reviews, and 47% read at least 6 before engaging **[S]** (https://clutch.co/resources/b2b-buyer-data). Clutch rejected about 32% of 70k+ reviews in 2025 as suspected fakes, so verified reviews carry weight **[S]** (https://clutch.co/resources/ongoing-commitment-trustworthy-reviews-2025). Clutch launched a **ChatGPT app on 2026-04-23**, putting its provider data inside AI buying conversations **[S]** (https://clutch.co/press-releases/b2b-custom-gpt-app). G2: 45% say review-site citations make them trust AI answers most **[S]**.
- **Founder-led content:** personal profiles get about 2.75× the impressions and 5× the engagement of company pages. Company page reach is down 60–66% since 2024 **[S]** (https://meet-lea.com/en/blog/linkedin-personal-profile-vs-company-page-engagement, https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/the-declining-reach-of-linkedin-company-pages). In the Edelman–LinkedIn 2024 study, 73% of decision-makers trust thought leadership more than marketing materials **[S, cited via https://connectsafely.ai/articles/founder-led-inbound-linkedin-strategy-2026]**.
- **Open source and public artefacts:** Wednesday uses 1K+ GitHub stars as proof. WeblineIndia publishes about 200 n8n templates and is a top-3 n8n Creator **[S]**. Innovatrix publishes ranking posts on dev.to **[S]**.
- **Legal and operational transparency:** registered entity number, IP-on-payment, overlap hours, staging from day 1, weekly demos (SingleBit, Ailoitte) **[S]**.
- **Guarantees and pilots:** fixed price with written scope and "anything off the list comes off the price" (Innovatrix). 30-day support (Xenotix, HouseofMVPs). Discovery before fixed quote (Ailoitte) **[S]**. **[I]** For TecHaust: a paid 2-week "automation pilot" (one workflow, fixed fee, with a measurable hours-saved KPI), credited against the retainer.
- **Named case studies:** anonymised cases are weaker **[I]**. Mitigate with metrics, client role and industry, architecture diagrams, short screen-recorded demos, and video testimonials on Clutch (clients can review without being named publicly).

## 4. Brand name: "TecHaust" vs Tech Australia

**Facts [S]:**
- **Tech Australia** (techaust.com.au) is a Melbourne managed-IT MSP with 2–10 staff. It always calls itself "Tech Australia"; "techaust" appears only in its domain. It holds the **facebook.com/techaust** handle. Its LinkedIn is /company/techaustralia (https://techaust.com.au/, https://www.facebook.com/techaust/).
- A plain search for "techaust" returns mixed results: Tech Australia's FB page and pages, alongside techaust.com.
- Searching "TecHaust Technologies Balurghat" also returns **Balurghat Technologies Ltd**, a listed company (BGTC:IN), which is a second near-collision.
- Google's Organization structured data (doc updated 2026-09-08) recommends `name`, `alternateName`, `legalName`, `url`, `logo`, `address`, `sameAs`, `vatID`/`taxID`. Google says `iso6523Code` and `naics` are used to **disambiguate organisations with similar names** (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization). For the site name, Google uses WebSite structured data plus on-page and off-site references. One site name per domain (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names).

**Recommendations [I]:**
1. **Keep "TecHaust".** The collision is minor. Different country, category (MSP vs software/AI studio) and spelling, and Tech Australia doesn't brand as "TechAust". A rename would waste existing equity, the domain and AMC client familiarity. Always use the full form **"TecHaust Technologies"** with the distinctive capital H, and consider a descriptor line such as "TecHaust Technologies, software & AI studio, India".
2. **Schema:** add an `Organization` (or `ProfessionalService`) JSON-LD with:
   - `name: "TecHaust Technologies"`, `alternateName: ["TecHaust"]` (don't add "Techaust" if that invites a merge with Tech Australia)
   - `legalName` (the proprietor's trade name as registered), `taxID`/`vatID` = GSTIN (Google calls this a trust signal), `address` (Balurghat, Dakshin Dinajpur, WB 733133, IN), `areaServed`
   - `naics: "541511"` (Custom Computer Programming Services), `foundingDate`, `founder` (Person with LinkedIn `sameAs`)
   - `sameAs` → LinkedIn company page, founder profiles, Clutch, GoodFirms, GitHub, Google Business Profile
   - `knowsAbout` (LLM automation, Zoho, Tally, n8n…), and `disambiguatingDescription` (schema.org property): "Indian software and AI automation studio; not affiliated with Tech Australia (Melbourne)"
   - Add a `WebSite` block with `name: "TecHaust Technologies"`.
3. **Content signals:** use "India", "Balurghat/West Bengal" and the service category in the title tag, H1 subline, footer and About page. Put the same NAP everywhere. Create a **Google Business Profile** (free) to anchor the entity geographically.
4. **Fix third-party data:** claim and correct ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, GoodFirms and Clutch listings. Change category from "digital marketing / advertising networks" to "software development / AI automation", and headcount to 2–10. Directories feed LLM answers. G2 says 85% view vendors more favourably when AI mentions them **[S]**, so wrong categories hurt AEO.
5. **Handles:** facebook.com/techaust is taken. Standardise on **@techausttech** or **@techausthq** across LinkedIn, X, GitHub, YouTube and Instagram for consistency **[I]**.
6. Watch out for misreadings: "TecHaust" can read as "tech-aust" (Australia) or "tec-haust" (exhaust). The capital-H wordmark and the "Technologies" suffix reduce both. A one-line origin story on the About page (if there is one) helps memorability **[I]**.
